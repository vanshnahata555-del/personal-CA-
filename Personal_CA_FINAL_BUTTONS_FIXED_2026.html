Employee NPS — Section 80CCD(2) in the New Tax Regime

For Tax Year 2026-27, the New Tax Regime permits a deduction for an employer's contribution to the employee's Tier-1 NPS account under section 80CCD(2), up to 14% of salary. Employee-paid contributions under 80CCD(1) and the additional 80CCD(1B) deduction are not available in the New Tax Regime.

For the Old Tax Regime, 80CCD(1) and 80CCD(1B) may apply subject to their limits, while 80CCD(2) has a 14% ceiling for Central/State Government employers and 10% for PSU/other employers.

Source: Official Income Tax Department AY 2026-27 guidance and CBDT ITR validation rules.

📜 Indian Contract Law — historical + current reference

Current position: The principal central statute remains the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Act No. 9 of 1872). India Code lists it as enacted on 25 April 1872 and enforced from 1 September 1872. There is no separate replacement "Indian Contract Act, 2025/2026" in the current central-law record.

The Act has evolved through amendments and the separation/repeal of subject areas. For example, Sale of Goods provisions were repealed from the Contract Act when the Sale of Goods Act, 1930 became the dedicated statute, and partnership provisions were moved into the Indian Partnership Act, 1932. The Mediation Act, 2023 also contains amendments to the Contract Act.

Core current topics covered: proposal and acceptance; communication and revocation; consideration; capacity; free consent; coercion; undue influence; fraud; misrepresentation; mistake; lawful object and consideration; void agreements; contingent contracts; performance; quasi-contractual relations; breach and compensation; indemnity and guarantee; bailment; pledge; agency; and other surviving provisions.

Primary source: India Code — Indian Contract Act, 1872. Open official consolidated record.

Cess & surcharge (both regimes)

Health & Education Cess: 4% on the tax amount, in all cases where tax is payable. Surcharge applies where total income exceeds ₹50 lakh, at rates up to 25% (New Regime) or 37% (Old Regime).
